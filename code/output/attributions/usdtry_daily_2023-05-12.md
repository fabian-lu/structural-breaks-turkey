## USD/TRY Structural Break Analysis - May 12, 2023

### What Changed
The Turkish lira experienced a significant **depreciation** against the US dollar on May 12, 2023:
- **Magnitude**: The exchange rate shifted upward by 0.16, representing approximately a **10.7% depreciation** of the lira (from ~19.37 to ~21.45 TRY per USD)
- **Volatility surge**: Standard deviation increased dramatically from 0.11 to 1.69, indicating much higher market uncertainty
- **Trend continuation**: The depreciating trend that existed before the break accelerated afterward

### Timing and Potential Causes
The May 12, 2023 date coincides with **Turkey's presidential and parliamentary elections** (held May 14, 2023), with the first round results announced around this period. Key factors likely include:

1. **Electoral uncertainty**: Markets typically react to political uncertainty, especially given concerns about Turkey's economic policy direction
2. **Monetary policy expectations**: Speculation about potential changes to Turkey's unconventional low interest rate policy under different electoral outcomes
3. **Pre-existing pressure**: The lira was already under depreciation pressure, and electoral uncertainty likely accelerated this trend

### Significance Assessment
- **Moderate confidence** (53.8%) suggests the break is statistically detectable but not overwhelmingly strong
- The **substantial increase in volatility** indicates this was a period of significant market stress and uncertainty
- The **persistent nature** of the new level (maintained 30 days post-break) suggests a fundamental shift rather than temporary volatility

This break represents a classic example of how political events can trigger currency market instability, particularly in emerging market currencies already facing economic pressures.
