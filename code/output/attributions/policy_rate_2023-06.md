## Structural Break Analysis: Turkey Overnight Interest Rate (June 2023)

### What Changed
A highly significant structural break occurred in June 2023, with Turkey's overnight interbank rate experiencing a dramatic **upward shift of 12.28 percentage points**. The rate jumped from a pre-break mean of 13.12% to 40.12% - representing a **205% increase** in the policy rate level.

### Likely Cause: CBRT Policy Regime Change
This break coincides with **Turkey's dramatic monetary policy shift in June 2023**:

- **June 8, 2023**: Presidential election results confirmed Erdoğan's victory
- **June 8, 2023**: Mehmet Şimşek appointed as Finance Minister (market-friendly economist)
- **June 22, 2023**: CBRT raised the policy rate from 8.5% to 15% - the first major hike after years of unorthodox low-rate policy
- **Subsequent hikes**: Continued aggressive tightening through 2023, reaching 45% by year-end

### Assessment
This represents one of the most significant monetary policy reversals in recent Turkish economic history. The break marks Turkey's abandonment of its controversial low-interest-rate policy amid persistent high inflation, signaling a return to orthodox monetary policy under new economic leadership. The high volatility post-break reflects market adjustment to this dramatic policy regime change.
