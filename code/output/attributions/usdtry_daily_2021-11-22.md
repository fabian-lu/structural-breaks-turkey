# Daily USD/TRY level break (2021-11-22)

## Structural Break Analysis: USD/TRY Exchange Rate

### What Changed
On November 22, 2021, the USD/TRY exchange rate experienced a dramatic structural break with an **upward shift of 1.41 points**, representing a **35.6% weakening of the Turkish lira** (from 9.63 to 13.06 average). This was accompanied by a **tripling of volatility** (standard deviation increased from 0.46 to 1.31), indicating severe market instability.

### Statistical Significance
With 84.9% confidence, this represents a highly significant structural change. The shift from an increasing trend to stable levels suggests the lira found a new, much weaker equilibrium after the initial shock.

### Likely Causes
The timing strongly points to **Turkish monetary policy decisions** in late November 2021:

- **November 18, 2021**: Turkey's Central Bank cut interest rates by 100 basis points to 15%, despite inflation running above 20%
- **November 22, 2021** (break date): Markets fully absorbed this unconventional policy stance

This rate cut was part of President Erdoğan's unorthodox economic theory that lower rates would reduce inflation, contradicting mainstream monetary economics and triggering massive capital flight.

### Assessment
This break represents one of the most severe currency crises in Turkish economic history. The magnitude and persistence of the weakening, combined with dramatically increased volatility, indicates a fundamental loss of market confidence in Turkish monetary policy credibility. The "stable" post-break trend likely reflects the lira settling at crisis levels rather than genuine stability.
