The break around **2018-06-01** marks a clear **downward regime shift** in TUR’s USD price.

- **What changed:** The ETF’s average price fell from about **$30.39 before the break to $19.96 after**, a drop of roughly **$10.43**, or **34%**. The detected break magnitude is **-11.73**, consistent with a large negative level shift. The trend also changed from **increasing before the break** to **decreasing after**, indicating not just a one-off drop but a persistent deterioration.
- **Statistical significance:** The confidence level of **76.9%** suggests a **moderately strong** structural break. It is not definitive at very high statistical thresholds, but the size of the move is economically significant, especially relative to the pre-break standard deviation of **3.33**.
- **Likely drivers:** This timing aligns closely with the **2018 Turkish currency and debt crisis**. Since TUR is priced in **USD**, it was hit by both weakness in Turkish equities and the sharp depreciation of the **Turkish lira**.
  - In **May 2018**, the lira came under severe pressure amid concerns about inflation, external debt, and central bank independence.
  - On **May 23, 2018**, Turkey’s central bank held an emergency rate hike, reflecting market stress.
  - The **June 24, 2018 Turkish elections** added political uncertainty, particularly around economic policy direction.
  - Later developments, including the **August 2018 U.S.–Turkey sanctions dispute over Pastor Andrew Brunson**, further intensified the selloff, though that occurred after the detected break.

Overall, the break likely reflects the market beginning to price in a sustained deterioration in Turkish macro conditions, especially lira weakness and policy credibility concerns. The evidence supports a meaningful downward shift, though the exact attribution should be viewed as macro-contextual rather than mechanically proven by the price data alone.
