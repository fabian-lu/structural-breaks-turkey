Around **2022-11-01**, TUR shows a clear **upward structural break** in USD terms.

- **What changed:** The ETF’s level shifted higher by about **$11.75**, with the short-window mean rising from **$19.10 before the break to $33.70 after** — roughly a **76% increase**. The pre-break series was relatively **stable**, while after the break it became **increasing** and more volatile, with standard deviation rising from **2.18 to 3.54**.

- **Statistical significance:** The break confidence is **72.2%**, which suggests a **moderately significant** regime change. It is meaningful, but not definitive enough to attribute to one cause without caution.

- **Likely drivers:** TUR reflects both **Turkish equity prices** and the **TRY/USD exchange rate**. In late 2022, Turkish equities rallied strongly as domestic investors used stocks as an inflation hedge amid very high inflation. Turkey’s annual inflation was reported at about **85.5% in October 2022**, released **Nov. 3, 2022**, near the break date. The Turkish central bank also continued its unorthodox easing cycle, cutting rates and later lowering the policy rate to **9% on Nov. 24, 2022**, which likely supported domestic equity speculation and liquidity.

- **Additional context:** The lira was relatively managed/stable during parts of late 2022 compared with earlier sharp depreciation, so the local equity rally translated more visibly into **USD gains** for TUR. A broader global risk rally after softer U.S. inflation data on **Nov. 10, 2022** may also have helped emerging-market assets, though that is a secondary and more speculative contributor.

Overall, the break appears to reflect a **sharp late-2022 re-rating of Turkish equities in USD terms**, driven mainly by a powerful local equity rally that outweighed currency weakness.
