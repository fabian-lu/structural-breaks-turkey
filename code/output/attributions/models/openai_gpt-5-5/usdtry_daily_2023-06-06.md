On **2023-06-06**, USD/TRY shows a clear **upward structural break**, meaning the Turkish lira weakened sharply versus the U.S. dollar.

- **What changed:** The 30-day average exchange rate rose from **19.80 to 24.77 TRY per USD**, an increase of about **4.97 TRY** or roughly **25%**. Since USD/TRY is quoted as lira per dollar, this represents a **significant depreciation of the Turkish lira**.
- **Volatility increased:** The standard deviation rose from **0.46 to 1.42**, indicating the post-break period was not only higher in level but also much more unstable.
- **Trend:** The rate was already increasing before the break, but after June 6 the upward move became much steeper and more volatile.

**Potential reasons:** The timing closely follows Turkey’s **May 2023 presidential election**, with the runoff on **2023-05-28**, after which markets appeared to price in a shift away from heavy FX management and toward more conventional policy. The appointment of **Mehmet Şimşek as finance minister on 2023-06-03** and later **Hafize Gaye Erkan as central bank governor on 2023-06-09** signaled possible policy normalization. A plausible interpretation is that authorities allowed more lira depreciation after the election, though this is partly inferential.

**Significance:** With **86.1% confidence** and a mean shift far larger than the pre-break volatility, this appears to be a **material and persistent regime change**, not just a one-day fluctuation. However, the confidence level is moderate rather than definitive, so it should be interpreted alongside market and policy context.
