## Structural Break Analysis: iShares MSCI Turkey ETF (TUR)

### What Changed
The TUR ETF experienced a **severe downward structural break** in June 2018, with the price level dropping by $11.73 (38.6% decline) from a pre-break mean of $30.39 to $19.96. The break also marked a regime shift from an increasing trend to a decreasing trend, with reduced volatility (standard deviation fell from 3.33 to 2.39).

### Timing and Likely Causes
The June 2018 break coincides with the **Turkish currency crisis** that intensified during this period:

- **May-August 2018**: The Turkish lira experienced a severe depreciation against the USD, losing over 40% of its value
- **June 2018 specifically**: President Erdoğan's re-election with expanded powers raised concerns about central bank independence and monetary policy
- **August 10, 2018**: The crisis peaked when the lira plummeted following U.S. sanctions and tariff threats over detained American pastor Andrew Brunson

### Significance Assessment
This is a **highly significant structural break** (76.9% confidence) representing:
- A fundamental shift in investor sentiment toward Turkish assets
- The dual impact of both local equity market decline and severe currency depreciation
- A persistent regime change rather than temporary volatility

The magnitude and persistence of this break reflects the severity of Turkey's economic and political challenges during this period, making it one of the most notable emerging market crises of 2018. The reduced post-break volatility suggests the market found a new, lower equilibrium level.
