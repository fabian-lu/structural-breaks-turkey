## USD/TRY Structural Break Analysis - October 18, 2023

### What Changed
The Turkish lira experienced a significant weakening against the US dollar, with the exchange rate shifting upward by approximately 1.17 TRY (from 27.27 to 28.44) - a 4.3% depreciation. This represents a substantial one-day adjustment in the currency pair.

### Key Observations
- **Magnitude**: The break shows a clear upward shift, indicating lira weakness
- **Volatility**: Standard deviation decreased from 0.37 to 0.29, suggesting the market found a new equilibrium level with reduced uncertainty
- **Trend Continuation**: The increasing trend persisted both before and after the break, indicating ongoing structural pressures

### Potential Causes
Given the timing (mid-October 2023), several factors likely contributed:

1. **Turkish Monetary Policy**: Turkey's unconventional monetary policies under President Erdogan, including resistance to interest rate increases despite high inflation, continued to pressure the lira

2. **Global Risk Environment**: October 2023 saw heightened geopolitical tensions, particularly the escalation of the Israel-Hamas conflict (began October 7, 2023), which typically drives investors toward safe-haven currencies like the USD

3. **US Dollar Strength**: The Federal Reserve's hawkish stance and elevated US interest rates during this period supported dollar strength against emerging market currencies

### Significance Assessment
With 58.6% confidence, this break represents a moderate-to-strong statistical signal. The 4.3% depreciation in a single day, combined with the sustained new level, suggests a meaningful shift in market fundamentals rather than temporary volatility. The reduced post-break volatility indicates market acceptance of the new exchange rate level.

This break reflects Turkey's ongoing currency challenges and vulnerability to external shocks during a period of global uncertainty.
