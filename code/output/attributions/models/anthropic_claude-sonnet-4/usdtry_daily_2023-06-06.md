## USD/TRY Structural Break Analysis - June 6, 2023

### What Changed
The Turkish lira experienced a **significant devaluation** against the US dollar on June 6, 2023:
- **Magnitude**: 25% weakening (mean shifted from 19.80 to 24.77 TRY/USD)
- **Volatility**: Tripled from 0.46 to 1.42 standard deviation
- **Trend**: Maintained upward trajectory but at a much higher level
- **Confidence**: 86.1% statistical significance

### Likely Cause: Turkish Presidential Election Outcome
This break coincides with **Turkey's presidential runoff election on May 28, 2023**, where President Erdoğan secured re-election. The currency adjustment likely reflects:

1. **Policy Continuity Concerns**: Markets anticipated continuation of Erdoğan's unconventional monetary policies, including resistance to interest rate increases despite high inflation

2. **Economic Policy Uncertainty**: Erdoğan's heterodox approach to monetary policy (favoring low rates during inflationary periods) created sustained market skepticism

3. **Delayed Market Reaction**: The ~1-week lag between election results and the structural break suggests initial market processing followed by decisive repositioning

### Significance Assessment
This represents a **major currency crisis event** with:
- Substantial wealth transfer impact for Turkish citizens and businesses
- Increased economic uncertainty (evidenced by tripled volatility)
- Persistent nature suggested by continued upward trend post-break

The high statistical confidence (86.1%) and large magnitude indicate this was a fundamental shift in market perception of Turkish economic prospects rather than temporary volatility.
