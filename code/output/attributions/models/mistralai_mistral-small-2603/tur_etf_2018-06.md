### **Structural Break Analysis: iShares MSCI Turkey ETF (TUR) – June 2018**

#### **1. What Changed?**
- **Magnitude & Direction:** The ETF experienced an **11.73-point downward shift** in its monthly closing price, transitioning from a **mean of $30.39 (pre-break)** to **$19.96 (post-break)**.
- **Volatility:** Standard deviation decreased from **3.33 to 2.39**, indicating reduced price fluctuations post-break.
- **Trend Reversal:** The trend shifted from **increasing (pre-break)** to **decreasing (post-break)**.

#### **2. Possible Causes (Timing & Statistical Evidence)**
The break aligns with a period of severe **Turkish economic and political turmoil**, particularly in **mid-2018**:
- **Currency Crisis:** The **Turkish lira (TRY) collapsed** in **May-August 2018**, losing **~40% of its value** against the USD.
- **Monetary Policy:** The **Central Bank of Turkey (CBRT) raised interest rates aggressively** (from **8% to 17.75%** by September 2018) in a failed attempt to stabilize the lira.
- **Geopolitical Tensions:** The U.S. **imposed sanctions** on Turkey in **August 2018** (due to detained U.S. pastor Andrew Brunson), worsening investor sentiment.
- **Domestic Instability:** Political uncertainty (e.g., **Erdogan’s influence over monetary policy**) contributed to market distrust.

#### **3. External Events Near the Break Date**
- **May 2018:** The lira began weakening sharply (TRY/USD moved from ~4.0 to ~4.5).
- **June 1, 2018 (Break Date):** The ETF’s structural break coincides with the **start of the lira’s freefall**.
- **August 2018:** U.S. sanctions and a **currency intervention failure** led to further lira depreciation.

#### **4. Significance of the Change**
- **Statistical Confidence (76.9%):** Moderate-high confidence in the break’s validity.
- **Economic Impact:** The **lira’s crash** directly affected TUR’s USD-denominated price, as Turkish equities (even when priced in lira) became far cheaper in dollar terms.
- **Market Sentiment:** Investors likely **exited Turkish assets** due to **currency risk, inflation fears, and policy instability**.

#### **5. Avoiding Speculation**
- The break is **consistent with known macroeconomic events** (lira crisis, rate hikes, sanctions).
- No need to invoke unrelated factors (e.g., corporate news) since the ETF tracks broad Turkish equity exposure.

### **Conclusion**
The **June 2018 structural break** in TUR reflects the **lira’s collapse and Turkey’s economic crisis**, leading to a **sharp, persistent decline** in the ETF’s USD-denominated price. The break is **economically justified** by the timing of the currency crisis and policy responses.
