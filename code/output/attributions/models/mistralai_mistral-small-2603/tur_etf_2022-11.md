### **Structural Break Analysis: iShares MSCI Turkey ETF (TUR) – November 2022**

#### **1. What Changed?**
- **Magnitude & Direction:** A significant upward shift of **11.75 USD** (≈61.5% increase) occurred on **2022-11-01**, transitioning from a stable mean of **$19.10** to an increasing trend with a mean of **$33.70**.
- **Volatility:** Post-break, volatility nearly doubled (standard deviation increased from **2.18 to 3.54**).

#### **2. Possible Causes (Based on Timing & Data)**
- **Currency Effect:** Since TUR tracks Turkish equities in USD, the break likely reflects **lira depreciation** (TRY/USD) combined with **local market gains**.
- **Macro/Policy Events (Late 2022):**
  - **2022 Turkish Lira Crisis (Dec 2021–Mar 2023):** The lira lost ~50% against the USD in 2022, with severe depreciation accelerating in **Oct–Nov 2022** (TRY hit record lows in **Dec 2022**).
  - **Turkey’s Unconventional Monetary Policy:** The Central Bank of Turkey (CBRT) cut interest rates aggressively in **Sept–Oct 2022** (from 14% to 10.5%) despite high inflation (~85% YoY), weakening the lira further.
  - **Global Risk Sentiment:** The **Nov 2022 U.S. midterm elections** and **China’s COVID reopening discussions** may have briefly supported EM sentiment, but the primary driver appears lira-related.

#### **3. External Events Near Break Date**
- **Key Dates:**
  - **2022-10-26:** CBRT slashed rates to **10.5%** (further weakening TRY).
  - **2022-11-01:** U.S. midterm elections (limited direct impact on TRY, but risk sentiment was fragile).
  - **2022-11-15:** TRY hit an all-time low (~18.5 TRY/USD).

#### **4. Significance of the Change**
- **Statistical:** The **72.2% confidence** suggests a high likelihood of a true structural break (not noise).
- **Economic:** The **61.5% jump in USD terms** reflects both:
  - **Local equity gains** (Istanbul Stock Exchange rebounded in late 2022).
  - **Currency devaluation** (TRY losses amplified USD-denominated returns).

#### **5. Conclusion**
The break was **primarily driven by lira depreciation** amid Turkey’s monetary policy divergence and inflation crisis, with secondary support from local equity performance. The timing aligns with **CBRT rate cuts in Oct–Nov 2022**, which triggered a sharp TRY selloff. The increase in volatility post-break suggests heightened uncertainty in Turkish markets.

*(Note: While global events coincided, the lira’s collapse was the dominant factor.)*
