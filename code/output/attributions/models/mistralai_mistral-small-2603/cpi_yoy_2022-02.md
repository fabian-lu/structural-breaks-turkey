### **Structural Break Analysis: Turkish Consumer Price Inflation (CPI) – February 2022**

#### **1. Description of the Change**
- **Date:** February 1, 2022
- **Magnitude:** A sharp **upward shift** of **38.42 percentage points** in year-on-year inflation.
- **Before Break (Jan 2022):**
  - Mean inflation: **16.14%**
  - Standard deviation: **7.92%**
  - Trend: **Increasing** (but at a moderate pace)
- **After Break (Feb 2022 onward):**
  - Mean inflation: **64.51%**
  - Standard deviation: **12.56%** (higher volatility)
  - Trend: **Decreasing** (but remaining at elevated levels)

#### **2. Possible Causes (Based on Timing & Data)**
The sudden surge in inflation aligns with several key economic and geopolitical developments:

- **Global Commodity Price Surge (2021-2022):**
  - Post-pandemic demand recovery and supply chain disruptions drove up global energy and food prices.
  - **Key Event:** Russia-Ukraine war (Feb 24, 2022) exacerbated commodity price spikes (oil, wheat, metals).

- **Turkey’s Monetary & Exchange Rate Policies:**
  - **Unconventional Rate Cuts (2021-2022):** The Central Bank of Turkey (CBRT) aggressively cut interest rates despite rising inflation, weakening the lira.
  - **Lira Depreciation:** The Turkish lira lost ~40% against the USD in 2021-2022, increasing import costs (especially for energy and intermediate goods).

- **Domestic Supply Shocks:**
  - **Energy Price Hikes:** Turkey imports most of its energy; global oil/gas price surges (post-COVID + Russia-Ukraine war) directly raised domestic fuel costs.
  - **Food Inflation:** Drought conditions in 2021-2022 reduced agricultural output, pushing food prices higher.

#### **3. External Events Near the Break Date**
- **Feb 24, 2022:** Russia invades Ukraine → Global commodity markets disrupted.
- **Jan 2022:** CBRT continued rate cuts despite inflation rising (from 36% in Dec 2021 to 64% in Feb 2022).
- **Dec 2021:** Turkish lira crisis (TRY lost ~50% in 2021).

#### **4. Significance of the Change**
- **Statistically Significant:** The break test confirms a **95% confidence** in a structural shift.
- **Economic Impact:**
  - Inflation accelerated from **~16% to ~65%**, a **4x increase** in just one month.
  - Higher volatility (std dev increased from **7.92% to 12.56%**) suggests unstable price dynamics.
  - The **decreasing trend post-break** may reflect temporary supply adjustments or base effects, but inflation remained historically high.

#### **5. Conclusion**
The structural break in **Feb 2022** was primarily driven by:
1. **Global commodity shocks** (Russia-Ukraine war, post-COVID demand).
2. **Lira depreciation** due to loose monetary policy.
3. **Domestic supply constraints** (energy, food).

The break was **highly significant**, marking a transition from moderate inflation to an inflationary crisis. Subsequent policy responses (e.g., rate hikes, FX interventions) had limited immediate impact, as inflation remained elevated for months.
