### **Structural Break Analysis: USD/TRY Exchange Rate (2021-11-22)**

#### **1. What Changed?**
- **Magnitude & Direction:** The USD/TRY exchange rate experienced a **sharp upward shift of 1.41 lira per USD** on **November 22, 2021**, marking a significant depreciation of the Turkish lira.
- **Before Break (30-day window):**
  - Mean: **9.63 TRY/USD**
  - Standard Deviation: **0.46** (low volatility)
  - Trend: **Increasing** (gradual depreciation)
- **After Break (30-day window):**
  - Mean: **13.06 TRY/USD** (a **35.6% increase** from pre-break levels)
  - Standard Deviation: **1.31** (high volatility)
  - Trend: **Stable** (no further sharp movements)

#### **2. Possible Causes (Based on Timing & Data)**
- **Monetary Policy & Inflation Concerns:**
  - Turkey was experiencing **high inflation (above 20% in late 2021)** and **unconventional monetary policy** (low interest rates despite inflation).
  - The **Central Bank of Turkey (CBRT) had cut rates aggressively** in previous months, weakening the lira.
- **Political & Economic Uncertainty:**
  - **President Erdogan’s unorthodox stance on interest rates** (prioritizing growth over inflation control) may have spooked investors.
  - **Geopolitical tensions** (e.g., rising energy costs due to global supply issues) could have contributed to capital outflows.

#### **3. External Events Near the Break Date**
- **November 18, 2021:** **CBRT unexpectedly cut interest rates by 100 bps** (to 15%), defying market expectations.
- **November 22, 2021:** **No major official announcement**, but **market sentiment was fragile** due to prior policy decisions.
- **Ongoing Context:**
  - **Global inflation pressures** (post-COVID supply chain issues).
  - **Turkey’s dwindling foreign reserves** (used to stabilize the lira).

#### **4. Significance of the Change**
- **Statistical Significance:** The **84.9% confidence level** suggests a **high probability** that this was a true structural break, not random noise.
- **Economic Impact:** The **35.6% depreciation** in a single day was **one of the sharpest moves in recent years**, indicating a **loss of investor confidence** in Turkey’s economic policies.

#### **Conclusion**
The **November 22, 2021, break** in USD/TRY was driven by **monetary policy uncertainty, high inflation, and political factors**, leading to a **sudden and sustained depreciation** of the lira. The **CBRT’s rate cuts** and **global inflation pressures** were likely key contributors.
