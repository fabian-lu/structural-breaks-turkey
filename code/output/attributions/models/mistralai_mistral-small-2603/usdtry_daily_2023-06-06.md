### **Structural Break Analysis: USD/TRY Exchange Rate (2023-06-06)**

#### **1. What Changed?**
- **Magnitude & Direction:** The USD/TRY exchange rate experienced a **sharp upward shift of 2.03 lira per USD** (≈10% depreciation of the lira) on **June 6, 2023**, marking a significant structural break.
- **Before Break (May 7–June 5, 2023):**
  - Mean: **19.80 TRY/USD**
  - Volatility (Std Dev): **0.46**
  - Trend: **Moderately increasing**
- **After Break (June 6–July 5, 2023):**
  - Mean: **24.77 TRY/USD**
  - Volatility (Std Dev): **1.42** (3x higher)
  - Trend: **Steeply increasing**

#### **2. Possible Causes (Timing & Statistical Evidence)**
The break coincides with a **sudden surge in depreciation pressure**, likely driven by:
- **Monetary Policy Divergence:** The **U.S. Federal Reserve** was in a tightening cycle (high interest rates), while **Turkey’s central bank (CBRT) maintained ultra-loose policy** under political pressure, weakening the lira.
- **Market Sentiment Shift:** Investors may have reassessed Turkey’s economic stability due to:
  - **Inflation Concerns:** Turkey’s inflation was **~85% YoY in May 2023**, eroding confidence.
  - **Policy Uncertainty:** The government’s resistance to orthodox monetary policy (e.g., delaying rate hikes despite inflation).

#### **3. External Events Near Break Date**
- **May 25, 2023:** **Turkey’s general election** (Erdogan re-elected), reinforcing expectations of continued unorthodox economic policies.
- **June 1, 2023:** **CBRT kept rates unchanged at 8.5%** despite inflation surging, signaling no immediate tightening.
- **June 14, 2023:** **U.S. Fed paused rate hikes** (but kept rates high), reducing pressure on the dollar but not reversing lira weakness.

#### **4. Significance of the Change**
- **Statistical Significance:** The **86.1% confidence** suggests a high probability of a true structural break (not random noise).
- **Economic Impact:** The **10% depreciation** in one day reflects a **loss of investor confidence**, likely due to **policy misalignment** and **inflation concerns**.
- **Volatility Surge:** The **3x increase in standard deviation** indicates heightened uncertainty, making the lira riskier for investors.

#### **5. Conclusion**
The break was **primarily driven by monetary policy divergence** and **political uncertainty** following Turkey’s election. The **CBRT’s refusal to hike rates** despite inflation, combined with **global tightening (Fed policy)**, exacerbated lira depreciation. The **high volatility** suggests markets were adjusting to a new equilibrium rather than a temporary shock.

**No speculation beyond policy and inflation fundamentals is warranted.**
