Based on the time series data and the economic context of Turkey, here is an analysis of the structural break in the overnight interbank interest rate:

### 1. Description of Change
* **Magnitude and Direction:** There was a massive, statistically significant upward shift of **12.28 percentage points** in the mean interest rate (with high confidence at 92.2%). 
* **Level Shift:** The average rate jumped from **13.12%** (with a downward trend and low volatility, Std Dev: 3.15) to **40.12%** (with an upward trend and significantly higher volatility, Std Dev: 8.84). 

### 2. Possible Causes and External Events
* **Macroeconomic/Policy Context:** The timing directly aligns with a major pivot in Turkey’s macroeconomic and monetary policy framework. Following the Turkish general elections in May 2023, the government and the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (CBRT) abandoned their long-standing policy of keeping interest rates artificially low despite soaring inflation.
* **Specific Event:** On **June 9, 2023**, Hafize Gaye Erkan was appointed as the new Governor of the CBRT, signaling a return to orthodox monetary policy. The central bank subsequently initiated an aggressive monetary tightening cycle, raising its policy rate sharply to combat inflation and stabilize the lira, which directly drove up interbank and call-money rates. *(Note: While the policy rate hike actions materialized fully in the meetings following this date, the structural break on June 1 reflects the immediate market repricing and policy stance shift).*

### 3. Significance Assessment
* **Significance:** This is a **major structural break** of high economic and statistical significance. A near-tripling of the baseline interest rate accompanied by a complete reversal of the trend (from decreasing to rapidly increasing) reflects a fundamental regime change in Turkish monetary policy rather than normal cyclical volatility.
