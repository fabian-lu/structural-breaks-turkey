Based on the statistical evidence and the timing of the event, here is an analysis of the structural break in the USD/TRY exchange rate on November 22, 2021:

### 1. Description of Change
* **Direction and Magnitude:** The USD/TRY exchange rate experienced a sharp upward shift of 1.41, representing a massive and rapid depreciation of the Turkish lira. 
* **Level Shift:** The 30-day moving average jumped from 9.63 *before* the break to 13.06 *after* the break.
* **Volatility and Trend:** Market volatility surged significantly; the standard deviation nearly tripled from 0.46 to 1.31. While the pre-break period showed a gradual upward trend, the post-break period reflected an extreme, unstable new baseline of currency devaluation.

### 2. Statistical Significance
With an 84.9% confidence level, the data indicates a strong, persistent regime shift rather than transient market noise. The permanence of the higher mean and elevated volatility confirms a fundamental destabilization of the exchange rate during this window.

### 3. Possible Causes and External Events
The timing of this structural break coincides with extreme and unconventional monetary policy decisions in Turkey:
* **Monetary Policy Decisions:** Throughout the autumn of 2021, under political pressure from President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (TBRT) aggressively cut interest rates despite soaring inflation (a policy stance contradicting standard economic theory). 
* **Specific Catalyst (Speculation/Context):** On November 18, 2021, the CBRT cut its policy rate by another 100 basis points to 15%. This monetary easing cycle triggered a panic sell-off in the lira, with the acute market fallout fully materializing in the exchange rate data by November 22, leading to the observed upward shift. 

### Summary
The structural break on November 22, 2021, marks the tipping point of a currency crisis driven by aggressive interest rate cuts amid high inflation. It resulted in an immediate, permanent devaluation of the Turkish lira of over 35% in average value and a tripling of market volatility.
