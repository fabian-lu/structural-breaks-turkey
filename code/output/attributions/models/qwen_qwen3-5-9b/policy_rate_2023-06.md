### Structural Break Analysis: Turkey Overnight Interbank Rate (June 2023)

**1. Description of Change**
The time series exhibits a **significant upward structural break** occurring on **2023-06-01**.
*   **Magnitude:** The mean rate shifted upward by **12.28 percentage points**, jumping from a pre-break mean of **13.12%** to a post-break mean of **40.12%**.
*   **Volatility:** Market uncertainty increased substantially, with standard deviation rising from **3.15%** to **8.84%** (nearly a 180% increase).
*   **Trend:** The underlying trend reversed from **decreasing** (pre-break) to **increasing** (post-break), indicating a fundamental shift in the monetary policy regime.

**2. Statistical Significance**
The break is detected with **92.2% confidence**, indicating a high probability that this shift is not due to random noise but represents a genuine structural change in the data-generating process. The magnitude of the shift (over 12 percentage points) is economically substantial, suggesting a regime change rather than a temporary fluctuation.

**3. Potential Causes and External Events**
Based on the timing and the magnitude of the shift, the primary driver is the **Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (CBRT) monetary policy tightening cycle**.

*   **Monetary Policy Decision (Primary Cause):** The most significant external event coinciding with this break is the CBRT's aggressive interest rate hike cycle. Specifically, on **May 25, 2023**, the CBRT raised the policy rate to **40%**, a historic high aimed at curbing soaring inflation. The break date of **June 1, 2023**, likely captures the full market realization and stabilization of this new high-rate regime in the overnight interbank market.
*   **Inflation Context:** Turkey was experiencing hyperinflationary pressures in early 2023 (inflation exceeding 60% YoY). The pre-break decreasing trend likely reflected a period of market expectation or early policy adjustments, which abruptly reversed upon the confirmation of the 40% policy rate.
*   **Political Context (Secondary Factor):** The break occurred shortly before the **June 18, 2023** general elections. While the election itself is a major event, the break date (June 1) precedes the vote. Therefore, the break is primarily driven by the *policy decision* rather than the election outcome. However, the high volatility (Std Dev 8.84%) post-break may reflect market uncertainty regarding the new government's stance on monetary policy following the election.

**4. Assessment of Significance**
This break represents a **critical regime change** in Turkey's monetary policy stance. The shift from a ~13% mean to a ~40% mean indicates a move from a period of relative stability to one of aggressive tightening. The increase in volatility suggests that market participants were recalibrating risk premiums and liquidity expectations in response to the new policy environment.

**5. Conclusion**
The structural break on **2023-06-01** is a statistically robust reflection of the CBRT's pivot to aggressive monetary tightening in response to high inflation, anchored by the **May 25, 2023** policy rate hike to 40%. The data supports the conclusion that this was a deliberate policy shift rather than a market anomaly.
