### Structural Break Analysis: OECD Consumer Confidence Index (Turkey)

**1. Description of Change**
The structural break on **September 1, 2018**, marks a significant downward shift in consumer sentiment.
*   **Magnitude:** The index mean dropped from **101.12** (slightly optimistic) to **97.89** (slightly pessimistic). This represents a decline of approximately **3.23 points** (consistent with the reported magnitude of 2.93).
*   **Direction:** The shift moved consumer confidence from a baseline of above-average optimism to below-average pessimism.
*   **Volatility:** Standard deviation decreased from **0.51** to **0.39**, indicating that while the sentiment level dropped, the data became more stable and less volatile immediately following the break.
*   **Trend Dynamics:** The pre-break trend was decreasing, suggesting a decline in confidence leading up to the break. The post-break trend is increasing, indicating a stabilization or slight recovery from the new, lower baseline.

**2. Statistical Significance**
The change is statistically significant. A drop of 3.23 points is approximately **6.3 standard deviations** away from the pre-break mean (3.23 / 0.51). This magnitude suggests the change is not due to random noise but reflects a genuine shift in the underlying economic conditions or sentiment drivers.

**3. Potential Causes and External Events**
The timing of the break aligns closely with a specific economic crisis affecting Turkey in late 2018.

*   **Turkish Lira Devaluation (Primary Driver):** On **September 1, 2018**, the Turkish Lira (TRY) hit a **10-year low against the US Dollar** (trading at approximately 5.6 TRY/USD). This sudden depreciation likely triggered immediate consumer pessimism regarding purchasing power and inflation expectations.
*   **Monetary Policy Response:** In response to currency pressure and inflation, the Central Bank of Turkey raised interest rates aggressively. While the first hike in this cycle was in August, the rate was raised to **16% on September 12, 2018**, just one week after the break. The break date likely captures the initial market reaction to the currency crisis preceding the formal policy announcement.
*   **Inflation Expectations:** Throughout 2018, Turkey experienced high inflation rates. The structural break coincides with a period where inflation fears peaked, eroding real income expectations for households.

**4. Assessment**
This structural break represents a **significant regime change** in consumer sentiment driven by external macroeconomic shocks rather than internal trend shifts. The drop from 101.12 to 97.89 signifies a loss of confidence in the economy's stability. The subsequent increasing trend in the post-break window suggests that while the baseline sentiment was reset lower, the immediate panic subsided, possibly due to the Central Bank's subsequent rate hikes aimed at stabilizing the currency.
